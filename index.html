      How places are decided

      Final places are by points: a win is 1, a draw ½, a loss 0. Players on the same points are separated by the Buchholz tie-break, the BH column in the standings.

      Buchholz adds up the final scores of all of a player's opponents. Of two players on 4 points, the one whose opponents went on to score well had the harder road, so they place higher.

      It is not something a player can play for, and it keeps changing until the last game: when an opponent from round 2 wins in round 6, your Buchholz rises too. So a player can move up or down the table without playing, and final places are not final until the last board in the section has finished.

      Players still level after Buchholz share the place. Where a trophy or medal depends on it, the chief arbiter decides and explains the decision to both families.

      The day

      • Games are in the Pavilion. Parents stay in the Village Hall. No parents and no phones in the Pavilion while games are on.
      • Sections: U8 on the arbiters' left, U10 on their right, U12 at the far end.
      • Illegal move: first a warning and a blue counter by the clock, second two minutes to the opponent, third the game is lost.
      • Lunch, tea, coffee and cake in the Village Hall. Card payments only.
      • Certificates: collect from the tables in the Village Hall any time.
